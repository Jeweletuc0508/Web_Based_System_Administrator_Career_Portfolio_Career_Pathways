01 / CAREER DEVELOPMENT

Your career pathway

Build experience step by step—from supporting users to managing and designing critical infrastructure.

01⌘

ENTRY LEVEL

IT Support Technician

Help users solve everyday computer, account, and connectivity problems while learning how an organization's systems work.

FOCUSHelp desk · Troubleshooting · User support
  • Set up computers and user accounts
  • Diagnose basic hardware and software issues
  • Document incidents and solutions
02▤

MID LEVEL

System Administrator

Maintain servers, operating systems, accounts, backups, and security so services remain available and reliable.

FOCUSServers · Automation · Security
  • Monitor system health and performance
  • Manage access, updates, and backups
  • Investigate incidents and restore services
03⌁

ADVANCED LEVEL

Infrastructure Engineer

Plan, improve, and automate complex infrastructure across networks, cloud platforms, and enterprise services.

FOCUSArchitecture · Cloud · Automation
  • Design resilient infrastructure
  • Automate repeatable administration tasks
  • Lead capacity and disaster-recovery planning

02 / PREPARATION

Skills & qualifications

A strong administrator combines technical knowledge with clear communication, careful planning, and continuous learning.

01

Operating systems

Install, configure, update, and troubleshoot Windows and Linux environments.

02

Networking fundamentals

Understand IP addresses, DNS, DHCP, routing, Wi-Fi, and connectivity checks.

03

Security awareness

Apply least privilege, patching, multi-factor authentication, and safe practices.

04

Backup & recovery

Protect important data and understand restore testing and recovery planning.

05

Scripting & automation

Use beginner scripts to reduce repetitive work and improve consistency.

06

Communication

Explain technical issues clearly, document changes, and work well with a team.

POSSIBLE QUALIFICATIONS IT / Computer Science coursework CompTIA A+ or Network+ Linux or Microsoft training Hands-on lab practice
